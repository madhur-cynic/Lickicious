Delivered ROAS

Count the parcels that arrive, not the orders placed.

Same campaign, two ways to count

Bulk pet-food campaigns look great on placed orders, then lose a share of them at the door. fastrr Ads feeds Meta and Google only the orders that were delivered and accepted, so the algorithm finds more Aditi, Rahul and Fatima, and fewer one-time COD refusers. A refused 9 kg parcel costs twice to ship. Pay After Delivery, risk rules and COD confirmation then cut RTO by up to 30%.
